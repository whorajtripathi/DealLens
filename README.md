🔍 DealLens – AI-Powered Product Deal Finder

📖 Description
DealLens is an AI-powered web application that helps users find the best online deals by uploading a product image or entering a product description. It uses Google Gemini AI to identify the product, generate an optimized search query, fetch products from online stores, and rank the best deals based on price, ratings, and overall value.

🚀 Project Flow

1. Upload a product image or enter a product description.
2. Image is uploaded to Cloudinary (if provided).
3. Google Gemini AI analyzes the product and extracts its details.
4. An optimized search query is generated.
5. Products are searched across online marketplaces using SerpAPI.
6. Gemini AI ranks the results based on relevance and value.
7. The best deals are displayed with a comparison view and stored in MongoDB for future access.
